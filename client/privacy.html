Privacy Policy.

Effective 23 October 2026

Amplify Studio is a small Nigerian creative studio. This policy explains, in plain language, how information is handled when you use this website or contact us.

Website information

We do not sell personal data. The chat widget and WhatsApp link only pass along the messages and contact details that visitors choose to send.

Google user data

Amplify Studio uses a private automation tool that connects only to the studio owner’s own Google account, including Google Sheets and Gmail. It is used to save business-lead research to the owner’s sheet and to prepare or send the owner’s own outreach emails.

Information is used only for these purposes, stays in the owner’s own account and database, and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or AI training.

Amplify Studio’s use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can request deletion of information by emailing samstudiohub@gmail.com. You can also revoke access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to samstudiohub@gmail.com.
